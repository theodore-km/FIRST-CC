## 📇 今日场景卡 (Daily Card)
**场景**：银行 — ICBC 仰光分行柜台：跨境汇款未到账，查单 + 中转行扣费 + 加急处理
（贴合你的中缅跨境结算业务：仰光付款 → 昆明收款方，ICBC 内部跨境）
**难度**：B1 → B1+　|　**预计用时**：35 分钟

**核心句型**（先读原句，再升级，每条大声读 3 遍）：
1. **跟进未到账汇款**
   - 原句：My money didn't arrive. The supplier didn't get it.
   - 进阶：I'd like to follow up on a cross-border remittance we sent last Tuesday — the funds still haven't been credited to the beneficiary's account.
2. **递凭据、请求查单**
   - 原句：Can you check where my money is?
   - 进阶：Here's the SWIFT copy with the reference number — could you trace the payment for me?
3. **处理中转行扣费**
   - 原句：The bank in the middle took some money.
   - 进阶：So the intermediary bank deducted a fee? For future remittances, can we have the charges borne on our side, so the full amount reaches the beneficiary?
4. **礼貌施压、要求加急**
   - 原句：Please do it fast, it's very urgent.
   - 进阶：Could you treat this as urgent? The supplier is holding the shipment until the funds arrive.

**实战对话脚本**
> 场景设定：你公司上周二通过 ICBC 仰光分行向昆明供应商汇出 USD 48,000，对方至今未收到货款，发货在即。你到柜台处理。
>
> **Teller**: Good morning, sir. What can I do for you today?
> **Theo**: Good morning. I'd like to follow up on a cross-border remittance we sent last Tuesday. The beneficiary still hasn't received the funds.
> **Teller**: I see. May I have the reference number?
> **Theo**: Sure. Here's the SWIFT copy — the reference number is at the top right.
> **Teller**: Thank you. Let me pull up the record… OK, the payment left our bank on the 14th. There's no return notice on our side.
> **Theo**: The supplier's bank mentioned a possible fee deduction by an intermediary bank. Is that common for USD payments?
> **Teller**: Yes. When a payment is routed through correspondent banks, a middle fee may be deducted along the way.
> **Theo**: I see. For future remittances, can we arrange for the charges to be borne on our side, so the full amount reaches the beneficiary?
> **Teller**: Certainly. We'll mark your remittance instructions as "OUR" — then all charges are paid by the sender.
> **Theo**: Good. And for this payment, could you send a trace request to the correspondent bank today?
> **Teller**: Of course. I'll submit an inquiry right away. It usually takes one to two working days to get a reply.
> **Theo**: Could you treat it as urgent? The supplier is holding the shipment until the funds arrive.
> **Teller**: No problem, I'll flag it as priority.
> **Theo**: One more thing — for our next payment, could your FX desk quote us a rate for converting USD to CNY?
> **Teller**: Sure, I'll ask a colleague from the FX desk to contact you this afternoon.
> **Theo**: Perfect. Could you confirm the trace request by email? Here's my business card.
> **Teller**: Certainly. You'll hear from us by tomorrow afternoon.

**发音 / 重音 / 连读提示**：
- remittance /rɪˈmɪtəns/ — 重音在 **MI**，和 remit 同源
- beneficiary /ˌbenɪˈfɪʃəri/ — 重音在 **FI**：be-ne-**FI**-cia-ry
- intermediary /ˌɪntəˈmiːdiəri/ — 重音在 **ME**（美式也读 /-ˈmiːdieri/）
- deducted /dɪˈdʌktɪd/ — 重音在 **DUC**；credited /ˈkredɪtɪd/ 注意 credit 这里是动词「入账」
- 连读：haven't received → ha-ven(t)-re-**CEIVED**；pull up the record → pull-u(p)-the record；treat it as urgent → trea-ti-ta-**SUR**-gent
- 句重音示范：The **FULL** a-**MOUNT** reaches the bene-**FI**-ciary.

**学习重点**：
1. 把「查一下」从 check 升级为 follow up on / trace / pull up the record —— 这三个是银行+供应商场景通用高频词。
2. credit 作动词 = 入账："The payment hasn't been credited." 邮件里也天天见。
3. OUR 汇款方式的地道说法：charges borne on our side（费用由我方承担、全额到账）。
